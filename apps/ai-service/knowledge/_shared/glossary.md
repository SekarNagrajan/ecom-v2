# Portal glossary

## Booking
A reservation of space on a sailing. Created in the booking wizard on [[cite:/app/booking|Booking]]. Acceptance, amendment, cancel and payment happen on booking screens — never from chat alone.

## VGM
Verified Gross Mass — the certified weight of a packed container. Submitted under [[cite:/app/vgm|VGM]] before vessel cut-off.

## SI
Shipping Instruction — cargo and party details used to issue the bill of lading. Managed under [[cite:/app/shipping-instruction|Shipping Instruction]].

## CRO
Container Release Order — authorises empty pickup when eligibility checks pass. Screen: [[cite:/app/cro|CRO]].

## B/L
Bill of Lading — the transport document issued after SI processing. Screen: [[cite:/app/bl|Bill of Lading]].

## DO
Delivery Order — release document for cargo delivery at destination. Screen: [[cite:/app/delivery-order|Delivery Order]].

## ARN
Arrival Notice — notice that cargo has arrived / is available. Screen: [[cite:/app/arrival-notice|Arrival Notice]].

## POL / POD
Port of Loading / Port of Discharge. Used across schedules, rates and booking master details.

## ETD / ETA
Estimated Time of Departure / Arrival for a sailing.

## How citations work
When the assistant mentions a screen it may append `[[cite:/app/route|Title]]`. Open that route in the portal to continue the task.

## Verified vs suggested
Answers that come from this guide are verified portal instructions. Anything marked as a suggestion or example may be AI-generated and should be checked on the live screen.
