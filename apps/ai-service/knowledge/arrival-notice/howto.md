# Arrival Notice (ARN) how-to

## Prerequisites
- Arrival Notice module access.
- Relevant B/L or consignment reference.

## View arrival notices
1. Open [[cite:/app/arrival-notice|Arrival Notice]].
2. Search by the references shown on the search panel.
3. Review arrival / availability details.

## Expected outcome
Arrival notice records for inbound cargo when published for your tenant.

## Assistant usage
Ask how to find an arrival notice. Use Assistant mode for live lookups if list tools are available for your account.
