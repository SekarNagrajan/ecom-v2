# Bill of Lading (B/L) how-to

## Prerequisites
- Shipping Instruction processed far enough for B/L issuance rules.
- B/L module access.

## Work with bills of lading
1. Open [[cite:/app/bl|Bill of Lading]].
2. Locate the B/L or batch print / MCN flows your tenant provides.
3. Review parties, cargo and charges where editable.
4. Print or complete actions only on the B/L screens.

## Expected outcome
B/L documents available for print / download according to status and privileges.

## Tips
- Draft vs final wording depends on carrier process — check the status on screen.
- Corrections usually require SI or amendment flows, not chat.

## Assistant usage
Ask what a B/L status means or how to open B/L screens. The assistant will not invent B/L numbers.
