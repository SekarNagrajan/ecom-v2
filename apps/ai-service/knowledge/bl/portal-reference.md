## Bill Of Lading status codes — what each status means

Meaning of every stored status code on a bill of lading. Use these exact words when explaining the state of a record; never guess a meaning for a status code.

- Status `I` on a bill of lading means "Released".
- Status `P` on a bill of lading means "Ready to print".
- Status `C` on a bill of lading means "Confirmed by Carrier".
- Status `A` on a bill of lading means "Approved by customer".
- Status `D` on a bill of lading means "Verify Copy".
- Status `S` on a bill of lading means "Submitted by customer".
- Status `*` on a bill of lading means "All".
