# Booking how-to

## Prerequisites
- Signed-in user with Booking (BKG) access.
- Know origin, destination, cargo-ready date and equipment needs (or pick them from schedules/rates).

## Create a booking
1. Open [[cite:/app/schedules|Schedules]] or [[cite:/app/rates|Rates]], pick a sailing, then Book — or open [[cite:/app/booking|Booking]] → New.
2. Complete master details (origin, delivery, cargo ready date, equipment).
3. Add parties and cargo lines.
4. Review the preview, then Submit on the booking screen.
5. The AI assistant can prefill the wizard but never submits.

## Expected outcome
A booking appears in the booking list with a reference. Status starts as Draft or Submitted depending on your tenant workflow.

## Common statuses
Draft, Submitted, Confirmed, Cancelled (exact labels follow the booking list screen).

## Validation tips
- Missing POL/POD or equipment blocks progress.
- Cargo-ready date must be realistic relative to ETD.
- Parties may be required before submit depending on tenant rules.

## What the assistant will not do
Submit, amend, cancel, approve or pay for a booking. Use the booking screen for those actions.
