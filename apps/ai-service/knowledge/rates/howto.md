# Rates and quotes how-to

## Prerequisites
- Lane (origin / destination) and equipment type when required.

## Search rates
1. Open [[cite:/app/rates|Rates]].
2. Enter origin, destination and container type.
3. Review indicative tariffs / published rates.
4. Request a quote when your tenant supports quote requests ([[cite:/app/quotes|Quotes]]).

## Expected outcome
Indicative rate lines for the lane. Quotes (if used) appear under Quotes for follow-up.

## Tips
- Rates are indicative unless your commercial team confirms a contract rate.
- Share or email rate results only through the portal share actions when available.

## Assistant usage
Ask for rates on a lane; the assistant looks up published tariffs and will not invent prices.
