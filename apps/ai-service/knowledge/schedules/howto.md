# Schedules how-to

## Prerequisites
- Origin and destination ports (codes or names your tenant recognises).
- Optional ETD window.

## Search sailings
1. Open [[cite:/app/schedules|Schedules]].
2. Enter origin, destination and date range.
3. Review results (transit, vessel, voyage, cut-offs when shown).
4. Use Book on a sailing to start a booking when booking is allowed.

## Expected outcome
A list or calendar of sailings matching the lane and dates.

## Tips
- Narrow the ETD window if too many results appear.
- Carbon estimate may be available from a sailing when the carbon module is enabled.
- Booking from a sailing may be blocked if the sailing is not bookable for your customer.

## Assistant usage
Ask for sailings between two ports; the assistant can search schedules and offer booking prefill — it does not submit bookings.
