# VGM how-to

## Prerequisites
- Container packed and weighing method known (Method 1 or Method 2 as applicable).
- VGM module access and booking/container reference.

## Submit VGM
1. Open [[cite:/app/vgm|VGM]].
2. Select or enter the booking / container.
3. Enter verified gross mass and weighing details.
4. Submit before the vessel VGM cut-off shown for the sailing.

## Expected outcome
A VGM declaration recorded for the container, visible in the VGM list with status.

## Tips
- Late VGM can block vessel loading — check cut-offs on schedules/booking.
- Weight units must match the form (kg vs lb as shown).

## Assistant usage
Ask how to submit VGM or what cut-off means. The assistant explains the steps; submission happens only on the VGM screen.
